### Present perfect
An unfinished moment/a consequence today/an experience

Has/have + pp
#### Keywords
- never/ever
- just
- since + start time
- for + duration
- always 
- yet

### Preterit
A finished moment/no connection to today

BV + ed/did not + BV/irregular
